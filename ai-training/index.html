Writers AI Training

Large language models have changed professional writing and content creation. AI does not replace writers, but it can make them more effective. Our Writers AI Training program draws on methods from our academic writing division and eCommerce strategy team to teach writers how to use AI tools well.

Prompt Engineering

AI output quality depends on the input. Generic prompts produce generic content. We teach you how to write prompts that control tone, style, and structure, and how to use AI for outlines, brainstorming research topics, and getting past writer's block. We also cover editing AI-generated text so it holds up for academic and corporate work.

Ethical AI Use

We cover AI detection and academic integrity, and teach educators, students, and professionals how to use AI as a research assistant without crossing into plagiarism. For clients using our dissertation services, we offer modules on using AI for data organization and literature review while keeping the final manuscript original and human-written.

AI Workflows for Business

For business clients, we show you how to build AI workflows that scale content production, from eCommerce product descriptions to first drafts for Generative AI SEO campaigns. This training also pairs well with our career advancement services, since AI skills are in demand. Review our corporate pricing options and enroll in a training cohort to get started.
