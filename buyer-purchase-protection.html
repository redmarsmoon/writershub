Purchase Protection FAQ

How long does the protection period last? expand_more
Buyer Purchase Protection covers you from payment until 14 days after final delivery. That gives you time to review the work and request changes.

No. We use PCI-DSS compliant gateways like Stripe and PayPal. We never store credit card numbers or other financial details on our servers.

If revisions do not address your initial requirements, you can escalate the case to our Satisfaction Team. We will either assign a more senior writer to the project or process a refund based on the scope of the issue.
