Dissertation Writing and Consulting

A doctoral dissertation requires original research, a full literature review, and sound methodology. WritersHub Plus provides academic writing services for graduate and doctoral candidates, from proposal through defense. Consultants are matched to your field, so a candidate in public health works with someone who has a health sciences background rather than a general researcher.

Chapter-by-Chapter Support

Our experts can help at any stage of your dissertation. This includes proposal drafting, research question formulation, and literature reviews built from current peer-reviewed research. For methodology, we support qualitative transcript coding (NVivo) and quantitative analysis (SPSS, SAS, R). You can bring in a completed chapter for review, or work with a consultant from a blank page through each of the four phases: proposal, data analysis, chapter writing, and final polish.

Research Design and Methodology

Consultants help build the methodology chapter itself, not just proofread it. For quantitative studies, that can include sample size justification, survey instrument design, and statistical modeling. For qualitative studies, we support interview protocol design and thematic coding. Mixed-methods projects get help integrating both strands into one coherent methodology chapter a committee can evaluate on its own terms. Sources cited in your literature review are checked against the original text, not just checked for correct formatting.

Editing and Formatting

Dissertations have stricter formatting rules than a standard essay: margins, pagination, and citation mapping. Our editors format your manuscript in APA 7th Edition, MLA 9th Edition, Chicago/Turabian, Harvard, or your university's specific style guide, and edit for structure and flow between chapters. Each chapter goes through our quality assurance process, including a plagiarism and AI-detection scan and a Turnitin or Copyscape similarity report you can share with your committee.

Master's Thesis Support

We work with master's thesis candidates as well as doctoral candidates. A master's thesis committee usually expects a narrower research question and a shorter literature review than a dissertation, and our consultants adjust the scope of support to match your program's requirements rather than applying a doctoral template to a master's-level project. If you are unsure which academic level applies to your program, tell us during intake and we will confirm before assigning a consultant.

Confidentiality

Your name, university, and department are not shared with the consultant assigned to your project. All files, drafts, and committee correspondence you upload stay within your private account in the order portal. This applies whether you are working with us for a single chapter review or ongoing support through the full dissertation.

Working With Your Consultant

You communicate directly and securely with your assigned consultant to share drafts, committee notes, and advisor feedback. When a committee returns a chapter with requested changes, send the feedback through the order portal and your consultant revises the affected sections rather than rewriting the whole document. Full copyright ownership of the finished work transfers to you on completion, so the dissertation is yours to submit and defend.

Defense Preparation

Beyond the written chapters, consultants can help you prepare for your proposal defense and final defense. That includes reviewing likely committee questions about your methodology and findings, and building presentation slides that summarize your research clearly within a limited defense window. Some candidates schedule a single defense-prep session; others work through several practice rounds before the actual date, depending on how comfortable they feel presenting the material.

After Graduation

Many doctoral clients also use our career coaching services to turn an academic CV into a resume for corporate or non-profit roles, along with LinkedIn profile help and interview prep.
