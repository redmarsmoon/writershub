LinkedIn Profile Optimization

Your LinkedIn profile is visible to recruiters at any time, unlike a resume you send once. WritersHub Plus applies what we know from digital marketing and career strategy to help your profile get found and read.

Headline Optimization

LinkedIn gives you 220 characters for your headline, and it appears next to your name in every search result, comment, and connection request. A headline that only repeats your job title wastes that space. We write one that states what you do, who you help, and a term recruiters actually search for, so a reader understands your value before they open the rest of your profile.

Keywords and Search Visibility

LinkedIn ranks profiles in search results based on keyword placement, profile completeness, and activity, the same way a search engine indexes a webpage. We use the same keyword research process we apply in Generative AI SEO work to find the terms recruiters search for in your field, whether that's a job title, a certification, or specific software, then place them in your headline, About section, skills list, and experience descriptions so you surface in more recruiter searches.

Writing Your About Section

Visibility gets you found. The About section, which allows up to 2,600 characters, is what convinces someone to reach out. We write it in first person, covering your background, core skills, and what you're looking for next, using the same writing standards as our writing services. We check it against your resume so both documents describe the same roles, dates, and achievements. If you're a recent graduate from our academic services, we highlight your research and publications alongside any internships or work experience.

Featured Section and Work Samples

The Featured section sits below your About summary and lets you pin posts, articles, links, and documents to the top of your profile. We help you decide what belongs there, a portfolio link, a published article, a certificate, or a case study relevant to the roles you want, so a recruiter sees evidence of your work in the first few seconds instead of scrolling through your full activity history.

Experience Section and Achievements

Under each job, most profiles list duties. We rewrite them as achievements using the STAR method, situation, task, action, result, with specific numbers wherever you have them: projects delivered, budget managed, team size, or a percentage improvement. Bullet points are kept short and scannable, since recruiters spend only a few seconds on each profile before deciding whether to keep reading.

Skills, Endorsements, and Recommendations

LinkedIn allows up to 50 skills on a profile, and you can pin your top three so they display first. We choose a skills list that matches the filters recruiters use in LinkedIn Recruiter searches, then guide you on requesting endorsements from former colleagues and managers so those skills carry weight. We also help draft outreach messages asking past supervisors or clients for written recommendations, since a short recommendation from someone in your field adds credibility that self-written content can't.

Networking and Content

As part of our career packages, we give you guidance on reaching out to industry contacts, requesting recommendations, joining relevant LinkedIn groups, and posting updates that keep your profile active. LinkedIn's search and feed algorithms favor profiles with regular activity, so we also suggest a simple posting schedule you can realistically keep up with.

Pairing With Resume Writing

A resume and a LinkedIn profile serve different purposes: a resume is tailored to one job posting, while LinkedIn is a standing page recruiters find on their own. When you order both through our job hunting services, we keep job titles, dates, and achievement numbers consistent across both while adjusting tone and length for each format.
