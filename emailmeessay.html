EmailMeEssay Is Now WritersHub Plus

EmailMeEssay provided academic support for years and has now joined WritersHub Plus. Clients get access to more Ph.D. experts, stronger security, and a full range of new academic writing services. If you had an account or an order in progress with EmailMeEssay, contact our support team through the client portal and they can help you reconnect it under a WritersHub Plus profile.

Essay and Research Support

Former EmailMeEssay clients still get fast essay writing. You can also order dissertations and research papers. Essay orders cover argumentative, narrative, descriptive, and admissions essays, along with case studies and lab reports for science courses.

We have writers across STEM, humanities, law, and medicine. Every order is human-written and original, and covered by our Buyer Purchase Protection. Citations follow APA, MLA, Chicago, or Harvard style, and writers can work from a grading rubric, lecture notes, or a professor's specific instructions.

If you already have a draft, you can order editing and proofreading instead of a full rewrite. That service checks grammar, sentence structure, argument flow, and citation formatting while keeping your original wording and ideas intact.

The dissertation team can take on individual chapters, including the literature review, methodology, or discussion section, or manage a full dissertation from proposal through final defense copy. Data work covers quantitative analysis, such as regression models, and qualitative analysis, such as coded interview transcripts.

Subjects and Assignment Types

Writers take on term papers, case studies, lab reports, book and article reviews, discussion board responses, and presentation slides with speaker notes, in addition to full essays and dissertations. Business students can request SWOT analyses and marketing plans. Nursing and health science students can request care plans and evidence-based practice papers tied to a specific clinical scenario.

If your assignment sheet includes a grading rubric, upload it with your order so your writer can match point allocations, not just the general topic. See how to place an order for a walkthrough of the submission form.

Career Services

WritersHub Plus also gives you access to career coaching and job hunting help, including resumes and cover letters. EmailMeEssay did not offer this before the merger, so it's a new option for returning clients. Resumes are built around the keywords in a specific job posting so they read correctly in applicant tracking systems, and cover letters are written to match.

We also offer LinkedIn profile optimization to help new graduates get noticed by recruiters.

If you used EmailMeEssay for a thesis or capstone project, you can move directly from that document into a resume and job search with the same account, instead of starting over with a different company.

What Hasn't Changed

The ordering process is the one you're used to. You submit instructions and a deadline, get matched with a writer in your subject area, and can request revisions if something needs adjustment. Turnaround still ranges from a few hours for a short paper to several weeks for a longer project.

Your writer is chosen based on subject and academic level, not simply availability. If your assignment requires a specific methodology, source type, or software, such as SPSS or NVivo for a data set, note that in your order instructions so it's factored in from the first draft instead of added during revisions.

Ordering and Security

Our client portal uses encryption and is covered by our Privacy Policy. You can message your assigned writer directly and anonymously through the portal. You can also upload source material, track order status, and review your full order history in one place.

If you're a returning EmailMeEssay client, welcome back. If you're new, see how it works and review our pricing. Contact us with any questions about the transition, or check our FAQ for answers about revisions, refunds, and deadlines.
