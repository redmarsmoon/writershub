How it works for each service

The same four steps apply to both academic writing and career services, with a different specialist and deliverable at each stage.

1. Submit your requirements

Go to Order Now and fill out the intake form. For academic work, that means academic level, page count, citation style, and deadline, plus any rubric or lecture notes you want the writer to follow. For career services, upload your current resume and the job descriptions you're targeting.

2. Pay and get matched

You get a quote based on our published pricing, pay through an encrypted gateway, and we assign a specialist with relevant background. A nursing dissertation goes to a writer with a nursing or public health background; a resume for a marketing role goes to a career strategist who has worked in marketing and eCommerce.

3. Work with your specialist

You get a client portal to message your writer or strategist, track progress, and answer questions as they come up, including on methodology for research-based work or on target roles for a LinkedIn profile or resume.

4. Quality check and delivery

Academic papers are checked against your instructions and run through plagiarism and AI-detection software before delivery, 100% human-written. Resumes and cover letters are tested against ATS scanners before delivery.

Once delivered, review the work. If it doesn't match your instructions, request a free revision under our Buyer Purchase Protection. Ready to start? See the full ordering guide.
