Essay Cave is Now WritersHub Plus

Essay Cave helped students meet deadlines for years. It has now merged into WritersHub Plus. Clients get access to a larger network of academic writers, enhanced Buyer Purchase Protection, and a full range of academic writing services. If you had an open order or an existing account with Essay Cave, our support team can help you reconnect it under a WritersHub Plus profile through the client portal. You do not need to resubmit instructions for work that was already in progress.

More Than Essays

We still handle fast essay writing, including argumentative, narrative, compare-and-contrast, and admissions essays. WritersHub Plus also gives you access to research papers. Our team includes Ph.D. specialists who handle data analysis, literature reviews, and research methodology across subjects such as psychology, business, nursing, and the social sciences.

Papers are formatted in APA, MLA, Chicago, or Harvard style, depending on what your course requires. If you already have a draft and only need feedback, you can order editing and proofreading instead of a full rewrite. That service checks grammar, structure, and citation accuracy while keeping your own wording and argument intact.

Graduate students can use our dissertation writing team for a proposal, a methodology chapter, or final edits. The team can also take on a single chapter, such as a literature review, or work through a full document from proposal to a defense-ready draft.

Data analysis support covers quantitative methods such as regression and ANOVA testing, as well as qualitative methods such as thematic coding and case study analysis, matched to what your committee or department expects.

Subjects and Assignment Types

Beyond standard essays, writers take on term papers, case studies, lab reports, book and article reviews, discussion board posts, and presentation slides with speaker notes. Business students can request SWOT analyses and marketing plans. Nursing and health science students can request care plans and evidence-based practice papers built around a specific clinical scenario.

If your assignment sheet includes a rubric, upload it with your order so your writer can match point allocations, not just the general topic. This matters most for longer assignments, where a missed rubric requirement can cost more points than a weak sentence.

Career Services Are Now Included

Former Essay Cave clients now have access to our career coaching services, including resumes and cover letters. Essay Cave did not offer career services on its own, so this is new if you're used to ordering essays only. In short, our writers can turn your academic and work history into a resume built for applicant tracking systems, along with a cover letter tailored to a specific job posting.

We also offer LinkedIn profile optimization to help new graduates get noticed by recruiters.

For students finishing a dissertation or thesis, this means you can move from your final academic document into a job search with the same account and support team, instead of starting over with a separate resume company.

What Hasn't Changed

The core of the service is the same. You submit instructions, get matched with a writer qualified in your subject, and can request revisions if a draft needs adjustment. Deadlines still range from a few hours for a short essay to several weeks for a longer research project or dissertation chapter.

Ordering and Security

When you place a new order, you'll use our client dashboard. Pricing is upfront with no hidden fees, and you can message your writer directly through an encrypted channel. You can also track order status, upload assignment sheets or source material, and ask your writer questions before the deadline instead of waiting until delivery to raise a concern.

Your information is protected under our Privacy Policy. Every document is original and human-written. If you're a returning client, thank you for sticking with us. If you're new, see how our process works or check our FAQ for common questions about revisions, deadlines, and refunds.
