How to Order

This page explains how to order academic writing and career services from WritersHub Plus.

Ordering an Academic Project

Start on the Order Now page and fill out the intake form with your academic level, formatting style (APA, MLA, Chicago), page count, and deadline. This works for a standard essay or a full research paper.

Upload any supporting materials at this step. For dissertation writing, include your approved proposal and faculty feedback. More detail upfront means fewer revisions later.

Ordering Career Services

For a resume rewrite or LinkedIn profile optimization, submit your current CV, links to your target job postings, and a summary of your career goals. For eCommerce strategy or Generative AI SEO, use the order form to request a consultation, and we will send a custom, itemized quote.

Payment and Expert Matching

After you submit your order, you get a quote based on our pricing. Payment runs through encrypted gateways. We assign your project directly to a matched expert, there is no bidding. You get access to a client portal to message your writer, track progress, and download your files. Every order is backed by our Buyer Purchase Protection. For more on the process, see how it works.
