How Pricing Works at WritersHub Plus

Pricing is upfront and there are no hidden fees. Use our academic writing services or career coaching, and you know the cost before you order.

Academic Writing Rates

Academic pricing depends on three things: academic level, deadline, and page count. A high school essay with a two-week deadline costs the least. A doctoral dissertation chapter on a 48-hour deadline costs more. Every order includes plagiarism reports, formatted title and reference pages, and revisions within the warranty period, at no extra charge, whether it is a full research paper or a short essay.

Job Hunting Packages

Career services are sold as packages, not individual fees. Our resume and cover letter package is built to get past ATS screening. Higher-tier packages add LinkedIn profile optimization, interview coaching, and salary negotiation help.

Custom Quotes for Business and eCommerce

Our eCommerce strategy and Generative AI SEO services are quoted per project. After a consultation on your scope, product descriptions, or SEO needs, we send an itemized quote.

Payment and Guarantees

Payments are processed through encrypted, secure gateways. Every order is covered by our Buyer Purchase Protection policy: if the work does not match your instructions, you get free revisions and a money-back guarantee.
