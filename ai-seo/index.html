What exactly is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)? expand_more
GEO is SEO built for AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Google's Gemini. Traditional SEO ranks your content in a list of links. GEO focuses on making your content the source these AI models cite when they generate an answer.
Why can't I just use my current SEO agency for AI search? expand_more
AI search needs different tools than traditional SEO. Most agencies still focus on keyword density and backlink volume. We specialize in GEO and other AI search methods most agencies don't offer.
How does AI SEO impact my traditional Google rankings? expand_more
The same strategies that build topical authority and technical structure for AI search also help with traditional Google rankings. Optimizing for AI search improves your content for all search engines.
Can AI SEO help me rank in ChatGPT and other conversational models? expand_more
Yes, this is a core part of our service. You can't buy your way into a ChatGPT response, but you can optimize your digital presence to make it more likely the AI cites your brand as a source.

Generative AI SEO

Search is changing as AI-driven search experiences grow. Old tactics like keyword stuffing and basic backlinks matter less now. Our Generative AI SEO service combines research methods from our academic research division with current AI search strategies to help your business stay visible.

How AI Search Differs From Traditional Search

Traditional SEO aims to get your page into the top results on a search engine results page, where a person clicks through to your site. AI search works differently. When someone asks ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, or Claude a question, the model reads several sources, summarizes an answer, and may or may not name where the information came from. Ranking well on Google no longer guarantees your brand gets mentioned in an AI-generated answer. Both goals depend on clear writing and solid topical coverage, but AI visibility also depends on how easily a model can extract and trust your content.

Structured Data and Technical Foundations

We add schema markup, structured data that search engines and AI crawlers can read directly, to your pages. This includes FAQ schema for question-and-answer content, Article schema for blog posts, and Organization schema so search engines and AI models have accurate, machine-readable facts about your business, such as its name, services, and location. Some sites now also publish an llms.txt file, a plain-text file that tells AI crawlers which pages to prioritize, similar to how a robots.txt file guides traditional search crawlers. We set this up on sites where it's supported.

Writing Content AI Models Can Extract

AI models tend to pull from content that states an answer clearly near the top of a page, rather than content that builds up to a conclusion after several paragraphs. We write in an answer-first format: a direct answer to the likely question in the first sentence or two, followed by supporting detail, examples, and context. We also use descriptive headers, numbered steps, and bullet lists, which are easier for both AI models and human readers to scan than long unbroken paragraphs.

Source Credibility and E-E-A-T

Google's quality guidelines describe E-E-A-T: experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trust. AI models draw on similar signals when deciding which sources to cite. We add author bylines with real credentials, link to original sources and data instead of restating secondhand claims, and keep publish and update dates visible, since AI systems tend to favor current, verifiable content over pages with no clear author or date.

Monitoring AI Search Performance

Standard tools like Google Search Console show your traditional rankings, but they don't show whether ChatGPT or Perplexity mentioned your brand. We track AI Overview appearances in Google and run test queries in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude to see whether and how your brand shows up in generated answers, then use that to decide what content to add or fix next.

Semantic Search and Entity Optimization

AI search algorithms read for topics, entities, and intent, not just keywords. We build entity maps for your brand and use NLP tools so Google's AI understands your eCommerce platform or website. We also write long-form content that answers the conversational questions people now ask AI chatbots, researched with the same care as a peer-reviewed publication or dissertation.

Traditional SEO Still Matters

AI search hasn't replaced traditional SEO, it adds to it. We still do keyword research, fix technical issues like page speed and mobile usability, build backlinks from relevant sites, and optimize on-page elements like title tags and internal linking. A site with weak technical SEO is harder for both search engines and AI crawlers to index in the first place, which limits AI visibility before content quality even becomes a factor.
