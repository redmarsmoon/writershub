VerifiMe: Identity Verification for Job Seekers and Businesses

Remote work and freelance platforms make it harder to prove who you are and what you've done. VerifiMe by WritersHub Plus verifies your identity and credentials, the same way our academic writing services guarantee originality and our eCommerce strategies are built on real results. The service combines automated document checks with a human review from a U.S.-based identity partner, so a verification result reflects more than a single algorithm's read of a scanned photo.

Why Verification Matters in Job Hunting

Employers want to reduce risk when reviewing applications. A strong resume from our career coaching team gets you the interview, and a VerifiMe badge shows your credentials are already checked. This matters most for international applicants and freelancers on platforms like Upwork or Fiverr, where distance can raise doubts for buyers. VerifiMe also works with our LinkedIn optimization service, so your verified badge shows on your profile.

What Documents and Steps Are Involved

The submission step asks for a government-issued photo ID, such as a passport, national ID card, or driver's license, along with a live selfie or short video for the liveness check that confirms a real person is submitting the request. Our matching step pairs each submission with a U.S.-based partner experienced in that country's document formats, which matters because security features like holographic overlays and microprinting vary from country to country. The verification step runs automated document analysis alongside a human review of the same documents, so a single algorithm error doesn't decide the outcome on its own. Most cases complete within the processing window listed on this page, though document types that are less common can take longer to confirm.

Serving Users Without Traditional Identity Infrastructure

Many freelance marketplaces, banks, and online platforms assume every user has a driver's license, a credit history, or a mailing address that shows up in a commercial database. That assumption leaves out a large number of people in developing countries who are otherwise qualified to work or bank online. VerifiMe routes each case through a U.S.-based human partner who reviews the documents directly, which gives a platform a verification result it can rely on even when an applicant's home country doesn't have the identity infrastructure some platforms expect by default.

How the VerifiMe Process Works

VerifiMe follows the data privacy standards in our Privacy Policy and screens against KYC and AML requirements where they apply. We verify your identity through government-issued documents, confirm degrees by contacting universities directly, and check employment history. For specialized backgrounds, such as those requesting research paper assistance, we also verify publications and licenses. Once verification is complete, you get a secure digital badge and a hosted verification page to share with employers.

Industries and Platforms That Rely on VerifiMe

Freelance marketplaces and gig platforms use VerifiMe to confirm a contractor is who they claim to be before a client commits to a project. Remote job boards use it to pre-screen international applicants. Academic institutions use it to confirm degrees and publication records, and financial services use it where KYC checks are part of onboarding a new user. In each case, the goal is the same: give a platform or employer a verification result that came from an actual document review, not just an unverified profile claim.
