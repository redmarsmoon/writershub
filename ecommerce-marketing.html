eCommerce Marketing and Conversion Copywriting

A good product is not enough on its own. WritersHub Plus offers eCommerce marketing services alongside our academic writing and career coaching work. We write product listings, category pages, blog content, landing pages, and email copy that turn visitors into buyers.

Product Descriptions

A product description is often the last thing a customer reads before buying. Our writers focus on benefits, not just features, using the same copywriting approach from our essay writing service. We also research keywords so your listings rank on Google and on marketplace search engines, which cuts down on how much you need to spend on ads. Bullet points cover specifications like size, material, and compatibility, while the opening lines answer the question a shopper is actually asking before they buy.

Platform-Specific Content: Amazon, Shopify, and Etsy

Each marketplace has its own content rules and ranking factors. Amazon's search algorithm weighs backend search terms, bullet points, and A+ Content modules, so a listing has to fit within Amazon's character limits and category-specific requirements. Shopify stores rely more on standard on-page SEO: meta titles, meta descriptions, and body copy that both Google and shoppers can read clearly. Etsy shoppers tend to search using longer, more descriptive phrases, and Etsy's search also weighs listing titles and tags differently than Amazon or Google. We write for these differences instead of reusing one version of copy across every platform.

Category and Collection Pages

Product pages get most of the attention, but category and collection pages matter for SEO too. A category page with only a grid of products and no text gives search engines little to index against. We write a short, useful introduction for each category page that includes the terms customers search for, which helps the page rank for broader searches beyond any single product name.

Image Alt Text and Mobile Layout

Search engines can't read a product photo, they read the alt text attached to it. We write descriptive alt text for your product images that includes relevant keywords without stuffing them in, which helps your listings show up in image search and improves accessibility for shoppers using screen readers. We also review how your product copy displays on mobile, since most shoppers browse on a phone, and long unbroken paragraphs get skipped when someone is scrolling with one thumb.

SEO Blog Content

Blog content reaches shoppers who aren't ready to buy yet but are searching for information: buying guides, comparison posts, and how-to articles related to your products. We research the questions your customers ask search engines and write posts that answer them directly, with internal links back to relevant product and category pages, which supports both SEO and on-site navigation toward a purchase.

Sales Funnels and Landing Pages

Sending traffic to a weak landing page wastes ad spend. Our Generative AI SEO and eCommerce teams write consistent copy across your ad, landing page, and checkout flow, so the message a customer clicks on matches what they see next. We also write variant headlines and calls to action so you can A/B test which version converts best for your audience.

Brand Storytelling and Email Marketing

Repeat customers come from trust in your brand. Much like our LinkedIn profile experts build personal brands, our team writes About Us pages, mission statements, and email campaigns for your store, including welcome series for new subscribers, abandoned cart follow-ups, and product launch announcements. We keep the tone consistent between your emails, your product pages, and your ads, since customers notice when a brand sounds different depending on where they encounter it.
